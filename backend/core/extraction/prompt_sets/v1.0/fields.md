# Fields

- urban_parcel_number: the planned urban parcel's number, as printed.
- block_ref: the block or plan zone the entity belongs to, as printed.
- planned_parcel_area_m2: the urban parcel's area.
- land_use: the land use designation, in the document's words or as its legend code.
- max_site_coverage_pct: the maximum site coverage (the coverage index).
- max_far: the maximum floor area ratio (the built-up index).
- max_height_m: the maximum building height in metres; only a height printed in metres (floors are not a height).
- max_floors: the maximum number of floors, in the local notation as printed.
- building_line_m: the distance of the building line from the regulation line or public area, in metres.
- setback_neighbours_m: the minimum distance from neighbouring parcels or buildings, in metres.
- parking_requirement: the parking rule, as printed.
- min_green_area_pct: the minimum share of green area.
- utilities: the utility connections stated for this scope, as printed.
- public_area_relation: how the parcel relates to public areas (regulation line, access), as printed.
- other_conditions, notes: any other rule or remark stated for the entity, one entry each, as printed.
